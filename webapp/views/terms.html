1. What Closedhand is

Closedhand is open-source software that runs on a computer you control, connects to the services you choose and carries out tasks through chat. These terms describe the local product and the public website and personal URL service. The software is also available under its repository licence.

2. Your account

Your dashboard password protects your dashboard. Your Closedhand account on closedhand.com holds your personal URL. You sign in with Google or Microsoft, and your Closedhand account stores your email and nothing else, or with an account number, and your Closedhand account stores no email at all. Your account number is the only way into the account made with that number: nobody can recover a lost account number or help you back in, closedhand.com included, so keep yours somewhere safe. Keep the computer running Closedhand and any sign-in account secure. You must be at least 16 years old to use the website service.

4. Connected services

When you connect third-party services (Google, Microsoft, Slack, Shopify, Meta, GitHub, etc.), Closedhand acts on your behalf using the permissions you grant. You can disconnect any service at any time from your dashboard. Closedhand only accesses your data when you ask, unless you have enabled features like Pulse that periodically check your services.

5. Mac access

If you give Closedhand access to your Mac, through Closedhand for Mac or the Closedhand Bridge app, you grant it access to your local files, applications, and terminal within the permission boundaries you set. You are responsible for what you instruct Closedhand to do. Review the permissions you grant and the actions you request. Confirmation depends on the action and your settings. You can turn individual permissions off or disconnect Bridge at any time.

6. Sandbox computer

The standard local setup includes Closedhand's sandbox computer, a separate space for browser and code tasks. Its files and website sessions can persist. You are responsible for the code and data you process there. Do not use it to attack or interfere with external systems.

7. Agents and routines

Agents can work on a question now or run tasks at times you choose. Their results are saved in your local task history and can be delivered in chat. Using an agent does not require a separate report. Unattended agents have a more restricted set of tools than the main chat.

8. Skills and MCP connections

You can extend Closedhand by installing community skills and MCP server connections. All installations are scanned for security risks before activation. However, we cannot guarantee the safety or quality of third-party skills and MCP servers. You install them at your own risk. We reserve the right to block or remove skills and MCP connections that pose security risks.

9. How Closedhand uses your data

Closedhand stores its application data on the computer running it. Selected LLM providers and connected services process the content needed for their work under their own terms. The public personal URL service stores the records needed to provide remote access. See our Privacy Policy for details.

10. AI-generated responses

Closedhand uses AI to understand your requests and generate responses. AI can make mistakes. You should review important actions before confirming them. Review your connection permissions and confirmation settings; not every action requires a separate confirmation.

11. Availability

Local chat, tasks and remote dashboard access require the computer running Closedhand to stay awake and online. Public website and connection services can also be unavailable during outages or maintenance.

13. Payment and billing

The local software is open source and free, and works with any LLM provider you choose, free ones included. The Closedhand subscription is optional and keeps the uncensored LLM Closedhand provides going after the free 14 days. The subscription renews every month, or every 6 or 12 months for less a month, until you cancel. If you never had the free 14 days, the monthly subscription's first 14 days are free instead: Polar takes your card, charges nothing until they end and emails you three days before. Polar (polar.sh) sells the subscription as merchant of record: Polar takes the payment, charges any tax, issues invoices and handles refunds under its own terms. You see the price in your currency before you pay. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and the subscription runs to the end of the period you've paid for; the time left is not refunded. We may change the price or what the subscription includes, and will tell you before a change affects a renewal.

Instead of the subscription by card, you can pay ahead in crypto, once: 3, 6 or 9 months. A plan paid ahead starts when the payment is confirmed and runs to the end of its last month; paying ahead again moves that end on. A plan paid ahead never renews by itself, and each of its months includes the same fair-use amount as a month of the subscription. Paying ahead in crypto is not offered while a subscription by card runs. An account made with an account number is paid ahead in crypto when it is made, and pays in crypto only, never by card.

A payment that charges at once starts what it pays for straight away: subscribing without free days first, paying ahead in crypto and carrying on past fair use. So you agree when you pay that you lose the right to cancel what you paid for once started, and the payment is not refunded, except where the law says otherwise. Deleting your Closedhand account ends the subscription or a plan paid ahead straight away, without a refund.

Crypto payments go through closedhand.com's own payment server, priced in US dollars and paid in the currency you choose there, at the rate it shows for the time the payment page is open. You pay the network's fee for sending. A payment that arrives late, short or after the page has expired doesn't count; write to support@closedhand.com and we'll sort it out.
